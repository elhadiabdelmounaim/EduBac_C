The user wants the Accueil notebook design generalized across all user-facing pages: warm paper surfaces, deep teal and ochre accents, prominent two-color lettering, and bold page typography. The site title is « EduBac », not « ETUDES BAC ».

**Why:** The user explicitly asked to generalize this design to every page after approving its use on Accueil, then corrected the title: « Le titre, c'est EduBac ».

**How to apply:** When adding or changing user-facing pages, follow the shared visual language and check both light and dark modes, mobile readability, and contrast. Preserve interactive workspace behavior and legibility of mathematical content.
