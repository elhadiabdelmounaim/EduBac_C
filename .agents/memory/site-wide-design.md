---
name: Site-wide visual direction
description: User-approved visual language and scope for EduBac pages
---
The user wants the Accueil notebook design generalized across all user-facing pages: warm paper surfaces, deep teal and ochre accents, prominent two-color ETUDES/BAC lettering, and bold page typography.

**Why:** The user explicitly asked to generalize this design to every page after approving its use on Accueil.

**How to apply:** When adding or changing user-facing pages, follow the shared visual language and check both light and dark modes, mobile readability, and contrast. Preserve interactive workspace behavior and legibility of mathematical content.
